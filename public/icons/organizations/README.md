# Organization logos

- `bytedance.svg`: [theSVG, brand color variant](https://thesvg.org/icon/bytedance), downloaded from [theSVG's asset repository](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/glincker/thesvg@main/public/icons/bytedance/default.svg).
- `huawei.svg`: [theSVG, brand color variant](https://thesvg.org/icon/huawei), downloaded from [theSVG's asset repository](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/glincker/thesvg@main/public/icons/huawei/default.svg).
- `xjtu.webp`: [Xi’an Jiaotong University's red emblem](https://vi.xjtu.edu.cn/images/a1-3jdxhred.png).
- `xidian.webp`: [Xidian University's blue transparent emblem, provided by note286/xdulogo](https://github.com/note286/xdulogo/blob/main/%E8%A5%BF%E7%94%B5%E6%96%B0%E6%A0%87%E5%BF%971-%E8%93%9D%E8%89%B2-%E9%80%8F%E6%98%8E.png).

The university images are resized to 160 × 160 and encoded as lossless WebP. Their original colors and proportions are preserved. Logos are served locally for reliable static exports. All logos retain their original colors in both themes. Company marks sit directly on the page background; university emblems use a white circular backing with a subtle border for contrast.
